MyPaisa Privacy Policy

Updated: 26 October 2026

Financial records are stored on your phone. Trial and Pro verification use limited server data. This policy describes both, including older supported versions.

1. Who is responsible

MyPaisa is developed and published by Ashish Kapoor, trading as Bhalanetra Labs, India. Privacy contact and Grievance Officer: Ashish Kapoor, kapoorashish213@gmail.com. Write with the subject MyPaisa privacy grievance for a privacy complaint. We aim to resolve grievances within one month, subject to any shorter applicable legal deadline.

MyPaisa is a personal finance tracker for adults in India. It does not provide banking, lending, trading, payment execution or personalised investment advisory services.

2. Information on your phone

Names and preferences you enter, accounts and masked account/card details, transactions, budgets, goals, investments, insurance, loans, salary and tax inputs, imported statement data and review items are held locally. Financial records use an encrypted SQLCipher database. App settings and local access proofs use protected storage. Android manages biometrics; MyPaisa does not receive your raw fingerprint or face data.

Files selected for import are processed locally. SMS access is optional and separately requested. With permission, MyPaisa reads SMS, including new messages in the background, to identify transaction alerts and prepare items for your confirmation. The parser is designed to ignore OTP, login, personal, promotional, reminder, balance-only and statement messages. SMS body text is not uploaded or saved. Parsed amount, type, merchant, sender label, masked account/card suffix, date, review state and a duplicate-detection fingerprint can be stored in the encrypted database.

MyPaisa does not request call logs, contacts, camera, microphone or location. Notification permission enables private local reminders. Boot access reschedules reminders. Network permission supports access verification, market-data requests and Play services. No advertising or behavioural analytics SDK is enabled.

3. Trial and purchase verification

Versions 1.5.0 and later do not require a separate MyPaisa account. To keep the original 30-day trial after reinstall, the app sends a SHA-256 hash derived from its app-scoped Android identifier. The raw Android identifier remains on the phone. The service keeps a further hashed trial key and original trial dates. Hashed identifiers and linkable records are treated as personal data, not assumed anonymous.

Google Play purchase receipts are sent over HTTPS to our access service for verification with Google Play. Payment card details are handled by Google Play, not MyPaisa. Receipt-only restoration does not persist the submitted raw purchase token in the current backend database. Older account-linked purchases may have saved tokens until deleted. Migrated Android versions keep a protected local receipt for refund reconciliation; it is excluded from financial backups.

Older versions use Google Apps Script and a private Google spreadsheet. Migrated versions use Cloudflare Workers and D1, with temporary legacy read-through to preserve original trial dates. Migrated versions also use Google Play Integrity for installation checks. Google assesses app/device integrity; our service checks integrity results, short-lived request hashes, installation public keys and request signatures. Private device keys stay in Android KeyStore. Stored service records include hashed trial/purchase state, installation public keys, original deadlines, revisions and short-lived replay/rate counters.

In the privacy-controls update, verification is enabled only after the in-app disclosure and an affirmative choice. You can change that choice in Settings > Privacy & legal. Disabling it stops new app-initiated verification requests; it does not erase existing server records or issue a new trial. Starting or restoring a purchase needs verification. Already verified access follows its existing proof and original trial deadline. Older installed versions may verify automatically before this control is available.

4. Service providers and other network requests

Google processes Play billing, Integrity and legacy access-service data. Cloudflare processes migrated access-service data. These providers may process IP addresses and technical connection/security logs when requests reach their systems. Their infrastructure and support may process data outside India under their applicable terms; we do not promise India-only hosting.

Market-data requests use public scheme codes, search inputs or ticker symbols with the configured providers, currently MFapi and Yahoo Finance in supported builds. Providers see network metadata such as IP addresses. Holding quantities, cost basis, financial databases, SMS and statement documents are not sent to the access service. Availability and permitted use of market data depend on the provider; a publicly reachable endpoint is not a licence to redistribute it.

If you deliberately open a bank, EPFO, support or other external website, that destination handles your interaction under its own policy. A backup can be saved to a cloud provider only if you choose that destination. Emailing support shares the contents of your email with our email provider. Please omit statements, passwords, PINs and purchase tokens.

Provider privacy information: Google https://policies.google.com/privacy and Cloudflare https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. We do not sell financial records or use access records for advertising. Necessary disclosures may be made to comply with applicable law or protect security.

5. Purposes, choices and legal basis

Local records are used to provide the tracking and calculation features you request. Limited verification data checks purchases, maintains original trial deadlines, prevents licence replay and helps secure the service. Support information is used to respond to your request. Permission-controlled features require the relevant Android permission and in-app choice.

We obtain consent where applicable law requires it. Contractual necessity, legal obligations or proportionate fraud/security interests may apply to particular processing where legally available. An Android permission or continued app use is not treated as blanket consent to unrelated processing. We do not add promotional email/SMS consent to permission or purchase flows.

MyPaisa is intended for adults aged 18 or above. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the privacy contact so we can assess removal. The app's content rating is not an age-verification mechanism.

6. Retention and backups

Local financial records remain until you delete them or remove the app's storage. Uninstalling removes private app storage but does not delete exported reports, chosen backup files or server verification records. Delete those exports separately through the destination you chose. We cannot recover a lost backup password or app PIN.

Legacy sign-in challenges expire after five minutes and sessions after 30 days. Cloudflare challenges expire after two minutes; consumed nonce and request-budget rows are eligible for removal by the daily cleanup after their recorded expiry. Expiry and physical cleanup are separate events. Failed maintenance can delay removal.

Original hashed trial/deadline and purchase ownership/revision history is retained while the access service supports those installations/purchases to prevent trial resets, ownership conflicts and replay of old proofs. This is an event-based retention criterion, not deletion at trial expiry. Installation public keys are retained for authenticated verification until a verified removal request or service retirement. Deleted legacy accounts retain only the limited continuity/anti-abuse history described here; raw saved receipt tokens and sessions are removed.

If a verified request cannot be fully erased because particular continuity, fraud-prevention, legal or dispute records are necessary, we explain the categories and reason, limit their use and reassess the need. Private recovery snapshots and provider backups can retain prior records until their applicable recovery retention ends; they are not used to reactivate a deleted account. A restore must reapply recorded deletion decisions. Ask the privacy contact for the retention information relevant to your request.

A deletion request does not automatically cancel or refund your Google Play purchase.

You may complain to the named Grievance Officer and exercise further rights available under applicable law. The deletion-request page is https://bhalanetralabs.in/mypaisa/delete-data/.

8. Security and incidents

Controls include encrypted private financial storage, HTTPS-only app networking, protected local keys, PIN hashing and retry delays, optional strong biometrics, authenticated password-encrypted backups, restricted PDF parsing and signed verification for migrated versions. Hardware-backed key protection depends on device capability. These controls reduce risk; they do not guarantee that a device or service cannot be compromised.

Report suspected security issues privately to kapoorashish213@gmail.com with the subject MyPaisa security report. Do not include customer data or credentials. We investigate, contain incidents and assess notifications to affected people, CERT-In or other authorities where required by applicable law. This statement does not claim an independent certification or completed regulatory audit.
