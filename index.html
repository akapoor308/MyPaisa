3. How Your Data is Stored

All financial information you enter — accounts, transactions, investments, budgets, loans, bills — is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This database is encrypted using SQLCipher (AES-256) with a unique encryption key generated on your device and secured using Android KeyStore. Financial records are not sent to the account service. An explicit password-encrypted backup includes the data and restore key at the destination you choose.

Your PIN is never stored in plain text. New PINs are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a random salt before being saved to Android DataStore Preferences. The App cannot recover your original PIN.

No cloud backup. The App sets android:allowBackup="false" and explicitly excludes sensitive files from Android's Auto Backup service. Your financial data is not automatically backed up to Google Drive or any other cloud service.

Retention: your data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or manually delete records within it. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

User-controlled backups

New backups are password-encrypted before being written to the file destination you choose. The archive includes the financial database and the key needed to restore it on another device. Keep your backup password safe; it cannot be recovered by us. Legacy ZIP backups are still accepted for restore, but are not password protected. Files may be stored with a cloud provider if you explicitly choose that provider in Android's file picker. Cached trial verification and Pro licences are not included in financial backups. Restoring a backup does not renew the original trial.

Automatic trial and Pro verification

MyPaisa 1.5.0 and later do not require Google sign-in or create an app account. To preserve your original 30-day trial after a reinstall, the App reads Android's app-scoped device identifier and sends a SHA-256 hash of it over HTTPS to our access service. The raw Android identifier stays on your phone. The service stores a further hashed record key and your original trial start and end dates in a private Google spreadsheet. This identifier is scoped to the App signing key, Android user profile and device; it is not your Google Play account or email address. A new device or factory reset may create a different trial identity.

The trial lasts 30 calendar days from its original start, including days when the App is uninstalled. Original dates from older App versions and restored backups may shorten the trial and cannot extend it. Trial history is retained to prevent normal reinstall resets.

Existing and new Google Play purchases are verified automatically when you open or return to the App. Purchase receipts are sent over HTTPS to our access service and checked with Google Play. Active lifetime purchases restore without MyPaisa sign-in; cancelled, refunded or pending purchases do not unlock Pro. The current restoration endpoint does not persist submitted purchase tokens in the database. Payment card details are handled by Google Play.

The service runs on Google Apps Script with a private Google spreadsheet. Only the service owner and explicitly authorized editors can access the spreadsheet. Google providers may process technical connection and service logs under their own policies. Application code does not log raw device identifiers, identity tokens, session credentials or purchase tokens. Financial records, holdings quantities, SMS messages and CAMS documents are not sent to this service.

Local cached access is encrypted with Android KeyStore and excluded from financial backups. Cached verification lasts at most 24 hours within the same device boot; reconnect after reboot or expiry. Uninstalling removes the local cache, but does not delete server trial history.

For users who signed in with versions before 1.5.0, existing hashed account trial history, purchase ownership history and saved verification records are retained for compatibility with those versions. Older sessions expire after 30 days. The new App removes local Google session credentials after carrying forward the original trial date and does not request Google identity tokens. To request deletion of legacy account sessions and saved purchase tokens, use the contact in Section 11 with the subject "MyPaisa account deletion". Identity verification is required; limited hashed trial dates and purchase ownership history remain to prevent abuse. You may also request deletion of device verification data through the same contact; we will explain any limited trial history that must be retained. Do not email financial statements, passwords or purchase tokens.

4. What We Do Not Collect

MyPaisa does not:

  • Upload your imported CAMS statements or SMS messages to our servers.
  • Upload your saved financial transactions, account balances or investment holdings to the account service.
  • Use advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network) or display advertisements.
  • Integrate analytics SDKs that profile your behaviour (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude).
  • Share any information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
  • Track your location.
  • Access your contacts, camera, or microphone. The app accesses files you explicitly select for import, backup, or restore.

5. Network Requests

The App makes outbound network requests for trial and Pro access verification described above and for publicly available market prices:

  • Mutual Fund NAV — queried from api.mfapi.in using only the publicly registered AMFI scheme code of a fund you have added. No account balances or unit counts are sent.
  • Stock Prices — queried from Yahoo Finance's public quote API using only the NSE/BSE ticker symbol you enter. No holding quantity or cost basis is sent.

All network communication uses HTTPS (TLS) exclusively. Plaintext HTTP traffic is blocked by the App's network security configuration.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies independent of MyPaisa.

6. SMS and Call Log Access

SMS-based money management is a core feature of MyPaisa. With your permission, the App reads SMS on your device, considers only transaction alerts, extracts fields such as amount, debit/credit type, merchant, date, and masked account/card suffix, and asks you to confirm before recording the transaction.

The App does not request Call Log access, send SMS messages, write SMS messages, or upload SMS content. OTP, login, security, personal, promotional, statement, reminder, and balance-only messages are ignored. SMS bodies are used only temporarily in memory for parsing; MyPaisa does not save or store SMS body text. Only parsed transaction fields are kept in the local encrypted database for review and duplicate prevention.

During update migration, any previously stored raw SMS review text or SMS previews are removed from the local encrypted database. Confirmed transaction records, account balances, budgets, and other user-entered finance data are preserved.

7. Security Measures

MyPaisa implements multiple layers of security appropriate for a financial application:

  • AES-256 encrypted database (SQLCipher) with a hardware-backed key stored in the Android KeyStore.
  • Optional PIN authentication with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashing and failed-attempt delays.
  • Biometric authentication via Android's BiometricPrompt API — raw biometric data is never accessible to the App.
  • HTTPS-only networking enforced through Android's Network Security Configuration.
  • ProGuard/R8 code obfuscation in release builds to resist reverse-engineering.
  • App screenshot prevention in the Android task switcher.
  • No automatic cloud backup of sensitive files; manual encrypted backups go to a destination you choose.

While we have taken reasonable precautions to protect your data, no security system is infallible. We recommend keeping your device PIN/password enabled and keeping the App up to date.
